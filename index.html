How we work

From first chat to launch day.

A clear path with no surprises. You see the work at every step and always know what comes next.

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    Tell us what is not working. We listen, ask questions, and say honestly whether software is the right fix.

  2. 02

    Scope & quote

    We map the root problem, agree on what to build first, and send a clear scope, timeline and price.

  3. 03

    Design

    You see the screens before we write code, while changes are still quick and cheap.

  4. 04

    Build & test

    We build in short cycles, show progress along the way, and test on real devices.

  5. 05

    Launch & care

    We launch, hand everything over, and stay around for fixes and the next improvements.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need. After a free first conversation we send a written scope and a quote, so you know the price before any work starts.

How long does it take?

A landing page usually takes one to two weeks. Web apps and custom systems take longer, so we plan them in stages and give you a timeline with the quote.

Do you work with clients outside Semarang?

Yes. Our office is in Semarang, and we work remotely with clients across Indonesia and abroad.

Can you help after launch?

Yes. We fix issues, keep things up to date, and build the next improvements when you are ready.

Do you only build software?

No. We also design and build websites, and make product videos and motion graphics through our video studio.
