A good-looking design is not enough.

Many projects stop at an attractive design while the business problem stays where it was. We start with that problem, then shape pages and content your customers can follow.

Design alone

  • The design looks good, but the problem remains.
  • Features are added without a clear reason.
  • Customers still do not understand what you offer.

With Difira

  • We study your business problem before designing anything.
  • Each feature is built to fix a specific part of how you work.
  • Pages and content are arranged so customers grasp your offer quickly.

Most of our work takes place before any code is written. We meet the people who will use the system, observe how they work, and keep asking questions until the root of the problem is clear.

01

Listening to users

We speak first with the cashiers, administrative staff, and business owners, and only then discuss features.

02

Finding the cause

A slow checkout, for example, may be caused by the way stock is recorded. For that reason, we examine the cause before we begin building.

03

Building what is needed

We start with the part that is needed most, put it into use, and add features once the system is actually being used.

Project stages.

Every project runs in five stages, and you can review the results of each one.

  1. 01

    Initial consultation

    Tell us about the problem you are facing. If it turns out that the problem does not require software, we will tell you so frankly.

  2. 02

    Scope and cost

    We set out what will be built first, how long it is expected to take, and what it will cost.

  3. 03

    Design

    You can review and approve the interface design before we begin writing code.

  4. 04

    Build

    We divide the work into several short cycles and show you the results on real devices.

  5. 05

    Launch and ongoing support

    After launch, we hand over all access to you and remain available whenever repairs are needed.

How much does a project cost?

The cost depends on the scope of work. After the consultation, we send a written quotation so that the cost is clear before work begins.

How long does it take?

A landing page is usually completed within one to two weeks. Larger systems take longer, so we divide them into several stages. The full schedule is set out in the quotation.

Does Difira Studio work with clients outside Semarang?

Yes. Our office is in Semarang, but most of our work is carried out online.

Is support available after launch?

Yes. We fix bugs, keep the system up to date, and add features when you need them.

Does Difira Studio only build software?

No. We also build websites, as well as product videos through our video studio.

Tell us what you need.

Send us a short message about your business and the problem you are facing. We will reply with the first step we propose to take.
